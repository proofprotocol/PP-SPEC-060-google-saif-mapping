# Contributing

PP-SPEC-060 is maintained as part of the Proof Protocol specification series.

Contributions may be submitted as issues or pull requests.

Contributions should preserve Proof Protocol's framework-agnostic architecture, distinguish concepts of Google SAIF from Proof Protocol concepts, identify sources clearly, respect upstream licensing and trademarks, avoid reproducing protected normative or legal text without permission, keep non-empirical requirements distinct from tested efficacy, and preserve the normative meaning of the specification.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution.
